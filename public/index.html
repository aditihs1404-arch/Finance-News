High impactFri 13 Nov

Chip sell-off sinks the Nasdaq 0.9%, but indexes log their best week since August

The Nasdaq fell 0.91% to 25,136.27 and the S&P 500 0.4% to 7,561.67, while the Dow edged up 50.46 points to 52,347.55. Technology was the worst of the 11 S&P sectors, down 1.7%. AMD reversed early gains to close down 8.75% at $534.06 as the AI-chip trade sold off. Even so, the S&P 500 gained 2.51% on the week, the Nasdaq 2.27% and the Dow 2.07%.

Why it matters: Money is leaving AI hardware, not stocks as a whole, so the index picture hides a sharp rotation; chip-heavy Taiwan and Korea are exposed when they reopen on Monday.

High impactFri 13 Nov

Nvidia slips below $4 trillion after HSBC downgrade

HSBC cut Nvidia to Hold, citing uncertainty after Jensen Huang's exit as CEO. The stock closed down about 2% near $161, its sixth straight weekly decline and the longest losing streak since 2012, with about $1.7 trillion of market value lost (CNBC). CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the market's biggest weight and the bellwether for AI spending; its slide sets up Tuesday's results as the week's main event for US tech and Asian suppliers.

High impactFri 13 Nov

Wholesale inflation at 6.5% keeps a December hike locked in

October producer prices rose 0.3% on the month, a touch below the 0.4% forecast, but 6.5% on the year. The 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.17% intraday, its highest since October, and CME FedWatch put the odds of a hike at the 9 Dec meeting at about 100%. The dollar index, around 103.2, was set for its best week since September.

Why it matters: Yields above 5% weigh on high-valuation tech and keep the dollar strong, which is pinning the yen near 160.

Medium impactFri 13 Nov

Yen stuck near 160 as Tokyo signals it may act

The yen hovered around 160 per dollar, close to its weakest since early September. Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama said the government would respond appropriately to currency volatility; Japan spent 15.4 trillion yen supporting the currency after it hit 163.99 in late July.

Why it matters: Intervention could jolt the dollar and Japanese exporters' shares when Tokyo reopens on Monday.

High impact16–20 Nov

Week ahead: Nvidia, Trump–Xi and the Anthropic IPO

Nvidia reports on Tuesday after the US close; analysts expect revenue of about $108.9 billion, up 91% on the year (LSEG). Trump meets Xi Jinping on 18–19 Nov, and CNBC says the Anthropic IPO caps the week.

Why it matters: Together these test the AI trade from three sides: earnings, chip export rules with China, and appetite for new AI stock.

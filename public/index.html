High impactTue 17 Nov

Nvidia tops forecasts in first report since Huang's exit

Fiscal third-quarter revenue rose 103% to $115.8 billion against about $109 billion expected, with adjusted earnings of $2.68 a share versus $2.47; data-centre sales were $107.6 billion. Nvidia guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130 billion (±3%, with an extra week and no China data-centre sales), lifted its next-fiscal-year outlook to about 75% growth and announced a $150 billion buyback. Gross margin is seen easing to 71–72% from 74.9%. Shares, which closed at $155.46, rose roughly 2–4% after hours.

Why it matters: The beat answers the question hanging over the AI trade after a month-long slide; it sets the tone for chip stocks in New York, Seoul and Taipei today.

High impactTue 17 Nov

Dow drops 849 points; S&P 500 at lowest close since July

The Dow fell 1.64% to 50,759.42, the S&P 500 1.03% to 7,340.53 and the Nasdaq 0.74% to 24,328.38. It was the first four-day losing streak for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq since September, driven by the fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation and yields above 5%.

Why it matters: The market went into Nvidia oversold; whether futures' rebound holds through the US open tonight shows if the selling is done.

Medium impactTue 17 Nov

Dollar at 19-month high, gold near one-year low, oil slips

The dollar index hit a fresh 19-month peak near 103.6, keeping gold near $3,869, close to its lowest in over a year. The 10-year yield held around 5.11% after October retail sales, and traders still price about a 95% chance of a December Fed hike (CME FedWatch). Oil eased, WTI ≈$85 and Brent ≈$92, on the IEA's fourth-quarter surplus forecast.

Why it matters: A strong dollar and 5% yields keep pressure on the yen, Asian currencies and gold; softer oil helps the inflation picture.

Medium impact18–19 Nov

Anthropic IPO prices tonight, trades Thursday as "ANT"

Anthropic is offering 825 million shares at $70–78, raising up to $64.4 billion and valuing it at up to about $1.4 trillion; reports say demand is about seven times the shares on offer and the price could be set above $80. Pricing comes Wednesday evening US time, with Nasdaq trading from Thursday.

Why it matters: The biggest IPO on record tests appetite for AI stocks right after Nvidia's numbers, and could draw money from other AI names.

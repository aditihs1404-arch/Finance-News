High impactWed 11 Nov

Nvidia sinks about 8% after hours on report the board may oust Huang

After Wednesday's close, the New York Times reported Jensen Huang had an extramarital relationship with a Nvidia executive, and the Wall Street Journal reported the board is preparing to remove him. Nvidia, which had closed up 2.9% at $173.73, was last down about 8% in extended trading; Nasdaq-100 futures fell about 1% and S&P 500 futures about 0.4%. Morgan Stanley also dropped Nvidia as a top pick.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the biggest weight in US indexes and anchors the AI chip chain in Taiwan, Korea and Japan; leadership uncertainty five days before its earnings puts the whole AI trade at risk today.

High impactWed 11 Nov

CoreWeave sparks an AI rally; Nasdaq up 1.6%

The S&P 500 rose 1.31% to 7,619.38, the Nasdaq 1.60% to 25,466.19 and the Dow 0.49% to 52,486.49, recovering Tuesday's CPI losses. CoreWeave surged about 25% (other outlets showed 16–19%) after beating forecasts, raising its outlook and saying it expects to be profitable on an adjusted basis in the first half of 2028. It gave back about 4% after hours with Nvidia.

Why it matters: The rally showed investors still want AI exposure when results back it up, but the overnight Nvidia news means much of Wednesday's gain is at risk.

Medium impactWed 11 Nov

Gold slips back below $4,000 as hike bets firm

Spot gold fell about 1.2% to roughly $3,984 an ounce after an early high near $4,067, as the hot inflation data and hawkish Fed comments kept the dollar firm and a December hike priced in. The bond market was closed for Veterans Day, so Treasuries did not trade; the 10-year ended Tuesday near 5%.

Why it matters: Rising rate expectations are pressuring gold and long-duration assets alike; Friday's PPI is the next test.

Medium impactWed 11 Nov

Oil steadies near two-month low as Hormuz attacks offset truce hopes

WTI was up about 0.4% near $86.40 late in the session, after settling at $86.04 on Tuesday (Brent $91.49). Attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz offset hopes for an Iran truce, and Treasury Secretary Bessent was moved to a military base over threats linked to Iran.

Why it matters: Oil is a key input to the inflation picture behind the Fed's hike talk; a renewed spike would add to rate pressure, while a lasting truce would ease it.

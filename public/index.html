High impactMon 23 Nov

Nasdaq jumps 6.5%, its best day since April 2025, as Nvidia soars 31%

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, and the stock rose more than 31%, adding roughly $1 trillion in market value. The Nasdaq closed up 6.51% at 24,089.56, the S&P 500 rose 4.73% to 7,237.17 and the Dow gained 1,447.98 points, or 3.01%, to 49,595.31 (CNBC, Yahoo Finance). All three had ended last week in correction.

Why it matters: The single biggest drag on the AI trade turned into its biggest lift in a day. But the board's own investigation and the reported SEC questions are still open, so the rebound needs follow-through.

Asia rallies at the open: Kospi up about 5%, Nikkei near 3%

In early Tuesday trade (Tokyo and Seoul opened at 07:00 WIB), South Korea's Kospi jumped more than 5% and Japan's Nikkei 225 rose about 2.8%, with the broader Topix up about 1.6% (CNBC; one Yahoo Finance snapshot had the Kospi up 5.6% and the Nikkei 3.5%). Tokyo was closed Monday for a holiday, so this is its first chance to react to both Friday's crash and Monday's rebound.

Why it matters: Korean memory makers and Japanese chip-equipment stocks were hit hardest by the AI rout. A strong close in Seoul would ease pressure before Thursday's expected Bank of Korea hike.

HPE beats and guides above estimates, but shares slip after hours

HPE reported adjusted earnings of $1.59 a share against a $1.49 estimate (LSEG) and forecast fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS of $5.05–$5.35 and revenue of $59.4–$63.1 billion, above consensus (CNBC). The stock fell about 5% in extended trading after rising nearly 13% in the regular session.

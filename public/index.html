High impactTue 27 Oct

Chips drive a second straight rally; Nasdaq up 1.18%

The S&P 500 gained 0.60% to 7,722.60 and the Nasdaq 1.18% to 26,477.81, while the Dow was nearly flat at 52,377.57. The SOX chip index rose about 3.6%, as the buzz around Anthropic's IPO filing kept the AI trade going ahead of Big Tech earnings.

Why it matters: Two good days after two weak weeks show money returning to AI names, but the gains rest on Alphabet and Microsoft results and the Fed tone. A miss on AI spending would reverse them quickly.

High impact28–29 Oct

The Fed decides tonight, with a hold expected and a December hike in play

The decision comes Wednesday at 2:00 pm ET (Thu 29 Oct, 01:00 WIB), with the target range at 3.75%–4%. A hold is the heavy favorite, but some watchers think Chairman Kevin Warsh could hint at further hikes, and CNBC cites about 83% odds of a December hike. The 10-year yield held near 4.99%.

Why it matters: Yields near 5% weigh on tech valuations. A hawkish tone would hit growth stocks and lift the dollar, pressing the yen and Asian currencies, while a softer one would extend the rally.

High impactWed 28 Oct

SK hynix posts record profit and plans a big payout; Korea opens higher

Third-quarter operating profit was 83.33 trillion won against 76.72 trillion expected, and revenue passed 100 trillion won for the first time at 104.62 trillion won. The company plans to return about 100 trillion won to shareholders, including at least 30 trillion won in dividends, and its shares rose over 3%. The Kospi opened up about 2.1% and the Nikkei about 0.7%; the Kospi had jumped roughly 3–4.5% on Tuesday.

Why it matters: It backs the AI memory demand story just before US Big Tech reports. The Kospi is still nearly 40% below its June peak, so moves are large and can reverse.

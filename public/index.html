Asia opens split: Japan rebounds, Korea extends losses

At the open the Nikkei 225 gained about 1.97% and the Topix about 2.8%, while the Kospi slipped about 0.69% and the Kosdaq about 0.87%. Friday's closes were brutal: the Nikkei fell 5.96% to 57,434.77 and the Kospi 6.02% to 5,059.69. These are early readings and will move through the session.

Wall Street ends a bruising week: Nasdaq and S&P bounce, Dow slips

The Nasdaq closed at 24,579.42 (+1.09%) and the S&P 500 at 7,376.86 (+0.31%); the Dow fell 237.33 points (−0.46%) to 51,285.73. Thursday's drop (S&P 500 −4.4%, Dow −1,800 points, Nasdaq −5.5% into correction) drove the S&P 500's worst week since April 2025. Sunday-night futures were mixed.

Strong jobs report lifts yields and the dollar, raising Fed-hike odds

The US added 165,000 jobs in October against about 59,000 expected. The 10-year yield rose to about 4.86%, the 2-year to about 4.52% and the 30-year to about 5.26%. Gold fell about 1% as the dollar and yields rose.

Why it matters: Strong data now means higher rates, which pressures growth and AI stocks and weighs on gold.

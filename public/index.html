High impactMon 9 Nov

Wall Street's best day since April: Dow +1,100 points, Nasdaq +2.7%

The Dow closed at 52,389.75 (+2.15%), the S&P 500 at 7,549.94 (+2.35%) and the Nasdaq at 25,253.14 (+2.74%). Chip stocks led. Energy was the only S&P 500 sector in the red as oil fell. The rally followed a week of AI-led losses and worries about a slowdown in data-centre spending.

Why it matters: It erases part of the sell-off, but a single strong day is not yet a trend; today's CPI can quickly change the mood.

Asia rebounds: Kospi jumps about 5% and is briefly halted, Nikkei up about 2.8%

In early trading the Kospi surged 5.09% (triggering a temporary halt), the Kosdaq rose 4.73%, the Nikkei 225 gained 2.78% and the Topix 1.77%, after last Friday's heavy falls (Kospi down about 6%+, Nikkei about 6%). High impactMon 9 Nov

AMD surges 13% while Nvidia slides 4% on board probe into Huang

AMD closed up about 13% on an Oppenheimer upgrade and its selection for a Google Cloud chip. Nvidia fell 4.27% after its board opened an independent probe into CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with a businesswoman US intelligence regards as a Chinese asset; Nvidia says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised. The Nasdaq still rose, showing the damage was largely confined to Nvidia.

Why it matters: Governance risk lands just before Nvidia's 17 Nov earnings, while money is rotating within the AI chip trade.

Medium impactMon 9 Nov

Oil drops about 5% as gold rises over 1%; Fed-hike odds stay near 81%

Brent sank by more than 4–5% to about $93–94, the lowest since early September, after Trump signalled new Iran strikes were unlikely while talks continue; Secretary of State Rubio said a breakthrough was not close. The 10-year yield ended near 4.88% and gold rose to about $4,138. CME FedWatch shows about an 81% chance of a December hike.

Why it matters: Cheaper oil eases inflation worries ahead of CPI, but yields near 4.9% still cap valuations.

The one thing to know · updated Tue 24 Nov, 00:45 WIB

Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO and its stock jumped roughly 30%, lifting the Nasdaq about 6% by midday in a sharp rebound from last week's AI rout.

  • Today: at midday in New York the Nasdaq was up about 6.3%, the S&P 500 about 4.5% (out of correction) and the Dow about 2.8%, with the chip index up about 15%. The 10-year yield is up about 4 bp near 4.95%; Brent is down about 1.3% near $91. In Asia, Kospi closed down 3.3% and Taiwan about 2% higher.
  • Nvidia's board says its independent investigation continues, and the SEC accounting questions reported Friday are not resolved, so the rebound can still fade. Korea's rate decision Thursday is expected to bring a quarter-point hike.
  • A holiday-shortened week: PCE inflation on Wednesday, US markets shut Thursday for Thanksgiving; a December Fed hike is about 96% priced.

High impactSun 22 Nov

Futures rebound sharply to start a short week

When trading reopened Sunday evening in New York, S&P 500 futures rose about 2%, Nasdaq-100 futures about 2.8% and Dow futures about 673 points, or 1.4% (CNBC; Reuters via Yahoo Finance). High impactFri 20 Nov

Recap: Nasdaq sank 5.3% as Nvidia fell 14% on SEC probe report

The Nasdaq lost 5.28% to 22,617.73 and turned negative for 2026; the S&P 500 fell 3.70% for a seventh straight loss and the Dow 1.93%. Nvidia slid about 14% after Reuters reported SEC staff are examining possible accounting issues (the SEC declined to comment), wiping out roughly $490 billion of value (Yahoo Finance, CNBC). Yahoo Finance also reports interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia has halted the affected chip shipments to Chinese firms and now models no revenue from them.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the biggest weight in the AI trade. Accounting doubts plus lost China sales hit confidence across the chip chain, from AMD and Micron to TSMC and Korean memory makers.

Week ahead: PCE inflation, Dell and HPE, then Thanksgiving

Wednesday's October PCE report, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, is the week's key data, with core seen up about 0.3% on the month; GDP data also lands Wednesday (Yahoo Finance). Why it matters: A December Fed hike is almost fully priced; a hot PCE would cement it and add pressure on stocks. Dell and HPE give a read on AI server demand after the Nvidia shock.

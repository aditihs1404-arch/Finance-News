Written Wed 11 Nov, 07:55 WIB. For anything newer, see "Since this morning" above.

High impactTue 10 Nov

Hot CPI lifts December hike bets; stocks give back part of Monday's rally

October consumer prices rose 0.4% on the month against a 0.3% forecast, and core prices rose 0.3% against 0.2%. The report had no annual figures because last year's government shutdown limited the data. The Dow closed down 0.30% at 52,231.89, the S&P 500 down 0.38% at 7,520.93 and the Nasdaq down 0.74% at 25,065.87. The S&P 500 is still about 4.1% below its record.

Why it matters: Inflation is not cooling, so the Fed looks set to hike in December, which weighs most on richly valued growth stocks.

High impactTue 10 Nov

10-year yield races back toward 5% as Waller backs a December hike

The 10-year Treasury yield rose about 13 basis points to roughly 4.99% at its high reading (another CNBC reading put it near 4.93%), the 2-year rose to about 4.74% and the 30-year to about 5.35%. Governor Waller said the Fed should raise rates in December even if inflation had cooled. Gold fell about 2.5% to around $4,035 as the dollar and yields rose.

Why it matters: A 10-year yield at 5% raises mortgage and corporate borrowing costs and pulls money away from stocks and gold.

High impactTue 10 Nov

Nvidia drops about 5.5% as the Huang probe widens

Nvidia was the main drag on the market, falling about 5.5% after a report that the alleged Chinese spy linked to CEO Jensen Huang helped $7.5 billion of chip shipments to China. Over two days its market value fell to about $4.07 trillion, the lowest since late March. Nasdaq-100 futures rose about 0.4% on Tuesday evening, US time.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the biggest weight in US indexes and the AI chip chain across Taiwan, Korea and Japan; its 17 Nov earnings now carry governance risk too.

Medium impactTue 10 Nov

Oil slides to about $89 Brent on Iran-peace hopes

Brent fell about 3% to near $89 a barrel and WTI to about $84.50, after Trump said the Iran war could end "fairly soon". Brent dipped below $90 for the first time since August during the session.

Why it matters: Cheaper oil is one of the few things that can ease the inflation pressure behind the Fed's hike talk; it hurts energy stocks.

Medium impact11–17 Nov

Week ahead: Cisco and Applied Materials, PPI Friday, Nvidia next Tuesday

Cisco, Applied Materials and CoreWeave report this week (one Yahoo report puts Cisco and Applied Materials on Thursday). Producer prices come on Friday, and Nvidia reports on 17 Nov. In Asia, the Kospi was down about 0.2% in early trade after Tuesday's 4.4% jump.

Why it matters: Applied Materials and Cisco are read-throughs for chip equipment and AI networking, and another hot inflation print would lock in a December hike.

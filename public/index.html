High impactFri 27 Nov

Wall Street ends the holiday week with a chip-led rally

In Friday's half-day session the Dow added 591.99 points, or 1.18%, to 50,774.94, the S&P 500 rose 1.6% to 7,297.49 and the Nasdaq gained 2.31% to 24,129.50 (Yahoo Finance). CNBC called it the Dow's best week since 2022, a sharp turn from the prior week, when the S&P 500 fell almost 9% in its worst week since April 2025.

Why it matters: The bounce came on thin holiday volume after a rapid correction; CNBC notes such rebounds have often not held, so Monday's session is the first real test.

High impactFri 27 Nov

Micron jumps, Nvidia lags on Southeast Asia shipment report

Micron rose more than 7% and AMD also led chip gains, while Nvidia flipped from a gain at the open to a decline after the Financial Times reported that Dell and Supermicro told buyers in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia that pending Nvidia AI server orders could not ship under new US export licensing rules (Yahoo Finance, CNBC). Nvidia is still up about 20% for the week after Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO.

Why it matters: Export curbs on Southeast Asia hit the data-centre build-out that Taiwan's server makers supply, and add to Nvidia's governance worries after S&P put its rating on watch.

High impactFri 27 Nov

Treasury yields jump as traders lock in a December Fed hike

The 10-year Treasury yield rose more than 8 basis points to about 4.93%, the 2-year to about 4.70% and the 30-year to about 5.32%, as markets looked ahead to next Friday's November payrolls (CNBC). The dollar index held near a 22-month high around 103.8, with the yen near 159.5 per dollar (CNBC, Yahoo Finance).

Why it matters: Higher yields and a strong dollar are a headwind for growth stocks and Asian currencies; a yen near 160 keeps Japanese intervention risk in view.

Medium impact27–29 Nov

Oil swings ahead of Sunday's OPEC+ meeting

Late Friday, Brent was down about 2.9% at $93.77 and WTI down nearly 1% at $82.86, giving back an early rally, though Brent was still on track for a weekly gain of about 1.4% (CNBC). OPEC+ is set to keep first-quarter 2027 output targets unchanged when it meets on Sunday, people familiar with the talks said (CNBC).

Why it matters: Oil feeds directly into the inflation picture behind the Fed's expected hike; a surprise from OPEC+ would move energy stocks on Monday.

Medium impactFri 27 Nov

Gold bounces from a 15-month low

Gold climbed nearly 3% to about $3,669 in futures trading on Friday after hitting its lowest level in 15 months earlier in the week, while silver rallied about 5% (CNBC, Yahoo Finance).

Why it matters: The rebound came despite higher yields, so it may be short-covering; the Fed hike outlook remains the main pressure on precious metals.

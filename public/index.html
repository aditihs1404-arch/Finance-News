High impact26–30 Oct

A busy week: Fed decision, Big Tech earnings and inflation data

About 160 S&P 500 companies report this week, including Alphabet and Microsoft (Wednesday after the close) and Amazon (Thursday). The Fed meets 27–28 Oct and is widely expected to hold, with Chair Warsh's press conference after the decision. September PCE inflation follows Thursday, with core expected near 3.1% a year. Intel and Eli Lilly also report. About 83% of S&P 500 companies so far have beaten earnings forecasts, per FactSet via CNBC.

Why it matters: Investors want proof that huge AI spending is paying off. A disappointing capital-spending outlook would extend last week's tech sell-off, and a hawkish Fed would add to it.

High impactFri 23 Oct

Oil at a six-month high keeps the Dow under pressure

Brent settled Friday at $116.72, up 3.1%, and WTI topped $100 on fears of renewed US-Iran conflict. The Dow fell 1.23% to 52,215.16, the S&P 500 0.52% and the Nasdaq 0.21%. Futures rose on Sunday evening, but no weekend oil level was found in CNBC or Yahoo coverage.

Why it matters: Oil above $100 feeds inflation and keeps rate-hike bets alive, and it hurts import-dependent Japan, Korea and Taiwan. Energy gains; airlines and consumer names lose.

Medium impactFri 23 Oct

The 10-year at 5% and a hike priced for December

The 10-year yield rose about 5 basis points on Friday to roughly 5.00%, the 2-year to about 4.69%. Traders see no move this week but roughly a 70–75% chance of a December quarter-point hike (sources differ slightly).

Why it matters: Yields at 5% weigh on growth and tech valuations, and a firmer dollar presses the yen and Asian currencies.

The AI trade is still shaky going into earnings

Last week's sell-off was driven by worries about how companies finance AI spending; Meta fell almost 7% on Thursday and about 1% more Friday. The Nasdaq nearly held flat on Friday, helped by a rare double upgrade of SpaceX to Buy. Alphabet is expected to show about 25% revenue growth, per LSEG.

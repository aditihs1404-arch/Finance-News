High impactWed 28 Oct

Alphabet sinks after a rare miss; Microsoft rises on Azure

Alphabet reported adjusted earnings of $2.67 a share against $3.02 expected and revenue of $127.58B against $128.22B. Search revenue of $63.35B (+12%) missed the $64.96B estimate and free cash flow was −$9.07B, though Google Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.7B and the $195B–$205B capex forecast was kept. Shares fell roughly 9%–10% after hours. Microsoft beat on earnings ($4.89 vs $4.73) and revenue ($92.28B vs $90.69B), with Azure up 48%, and rose about 2%–4% after hours.

Why it matters: The market is rewarding AI spending that shows up as cloud sales and punishing the opposite. Alphabet's cash burn and search miss pulled Nasdaq-100 futures down about 1.2%, so the open at 20:30 WIB is the first test of the three-day rally.

High impactWed 28 Oct

Fed holds at 3.75%–4% with two dissents for a hike; December hike odds near 85%

The vote was 10–2, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack preferring a quarter-point increase. Chairman Kevin Warsh said politics played "no role" and that he does not believe in forward guidance absent a crisis. The 10-year yield rose about 5 basis points to near 5.05% and the 2-year to about 4.72%–4.78%; CME FedWatch shows nearly an 85% chance of a December hike.

Why it matters: Two dissenters for a hike and a 10-year yield above 5% keep pressure on growth and tech valuations, and a stronger dollar would hurt the yen and Asian currencies. Stocks still closed higher, so the market is betting on earnings over rates.

High impactThu 29 Oct

Samsung reports today after a 9% run; SK hynix has set a high bar

Samsung Electronics rose about 9% to 242,000 won on Wednesday ahead of results. Its October guidance pointed to third-quarter revenue of 195 trillion won and operating profit of 107.4 trillion won, and investors want details on its shareholder returns. The Kospi closed Wednesday up about 6.6% at 6,012.44 after SK hynix's record profit, and the Nikkei rose about 1.1% to 63,954.19. The Kospi remains close to 40% below its June peak.

Why it matters: Samsung is the next check on the AI memory story that drove Asia this week, while Alphabet's slide may cool chip sentiment at today's open. Moves in Korea are large and can reverse quickly.

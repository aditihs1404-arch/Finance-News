High impactMon 16 Nov

Tech sell-off deepens: Nasdaq −2.5%, S&P 500 down a third day

The S&P 500 fell 1.92% and the Nasdaq 2.49%, both a third straight loss, while the Dow shed 739 points. Investors weighed Nvidia's leadership crisis, Treasury yields above 5% and new US chip curbs. The VIX fear gauge jumped as much as 25% to a high of 24.05.

Why it matters: The selling has spread from Nvidia to the wider AI trade, so tonight's results decide whether the slide steadies or deepens.

High impactTue 17 Nov

Nvidia results tonight, with interim CEO Kress at the helm

Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter results after the bell Tuesday. Consensus is earnings of $2.47 a share on revenue of about $109.15 billion (Bloomberg). The stock closed Monday at $156.14 and is on pace for its biggest monthly drop since April 2022. Investors want to know whether it keeps its outlook for roughly 70% growth next fiscal year.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the market's biggest weight and the gauge of AI spending; its numbers will move chip stocks in the US, Taiwan and Korea on Wednesday.

High impactMon 16 Nov

US moves to block remote access to advanced AI chips

The Commerce Department issued an interim rule aimed at stopping companies from reaching advanced AI chips remotely to get around export controls. Analysts cut price targets on Nvidia, and the rule could shrink its addressable market.

Why it matters: It tightens the China chip squeeze right before Nvidia reports and the Trump–Xi meeting on 18–19 Nov.

Medium impactMon 16 Nov

Oil drops 3.5% as Gulf exports recover; gold at one-year low

Brent settled at $92.44 and WTI at $85.39 after the IEA forecast a supply surplus in the fourth quarter as Hormuz shipments near pre-war levels. Gold fell to about $3,852, its lowest in over a year, hit by high yields and a dollar index near 103.6.

Why it matters: Cheaper oil eases inflation worries, but a firm dollar and 5% yields keep pressure on gold, emerging markets and the yen, near 159.5 per dollar.

High impactThu 26 Nov

Futures rise as Wall Street heads into a half-day finish to a brutal week

Just after 6 p.m. ET Thursday, S&P 500 futures were up 0.7%, Nasdaq-100 futures 0.6% and Dow futures 277 points, or 0.6% (CNBC). Earlier in the week the Nasdaq plunged more than 5%, and Wednesday the Dow erased a 1,100-point loss to close up 0.33% while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq ended lower (CNBC, Yahoo Finance).

Why it matters: Friday's session is short and thin, which can exaggerate moves in either direction; a firm close would ease fears that the Nasdaq-100 is sliding into a bear market.

High impactThu 26 Nov

S&P puts Nvidia's AA rating on watch for a possible downgrade

S&P Global Ratings placed Nvidia on CreditWatch negative over governance and "key man" risks after CEO Jensen Huang's departure and reinstatement, flagging a likely one-notch cut to AA- within 90 days; Moody's kept its Aa1 rating stable (CNBC, Yahoo Finance). Nvidia fell almost 4% on Wednesday.

Why it matters: Nvidia trades for the first time since the news on Friday; it adds a governance worry to an AI sell-off that has hit memory and chip names hardest, including Samsung, SK Hynix and TSMC.

Asia extends its chip-led rebound into Friday

On Thursday the Nikkei rose 0.9% to 57,499.16 and the Kospi added 0.3% after Wednesday's 6.2% plunge, while Taiwan led with a gain of about 2.5% (Yahoo Finance). Friday's open was higher again: Nikkei +0.3%, Topix +0.78%, Kospi +0.6% (CNBC).

Medium impactWed 25 Nov

Hot core PCE keeps a December Fed hike almost fully priced

October core PCE rose 3.3% on the year, a tenth above forecast, yet Treasurys rallied as investors sought safety, pulling the 10-year yield to about 4.82–4.87% (CNBC).

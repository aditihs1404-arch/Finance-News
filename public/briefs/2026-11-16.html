High impact17 Nov

Nvidia's first results without Jensen Huang land Tuesday

Nvidia reports third-quarter results after the US close on Tuesday (Wednesday morning WIB). Analysts polled by LSEG expect revenue of $109.08 billion and earnings of $2.47 a share. It is the first report in the company's 27 years as a public company without Huang as CEO. The stock fell 13% last week, its worst week since April 2025, and options imply a move of about 8% either way.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the market's biggest weight and the gauge of AI spending; its numbers will steer chip stocks in the US, Taiwan and Korea.

High impactSun 15 Nov

Bessent gives Nvidia 10 days to answer on chips reaching China

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called on Nvidia to explain whether it knowingly let its most advanced AI chips reach China, and wants answers from the company and its internal investigation into Huang's conduct within 10 days.

Why it matters: It adds regulatory risk to Nvidia just before earnings and ahead of the Trump–Xi meeting, where chip export rules are on the table.

High impactSun 15 Nov

Futures rebound after last week's AI sell-off

Nasdaq-100 futures rose 0.6% and S&P 500 futures 0.3% on Sunday evening US time as traders braced for Nvidia and the Anthropic IPO. On Friday the Nasdaq fell 0.9% and tech was the worst S&P sector, though all three indexes still had their best week since August.

Why it matters: Buyers are stepping back into tech, but the rebound is small next to last week's chip losses and depends on Tuesday's numbers.

Medium impactMon 16 Nov

Japan's economy unexpectedly shrinks; Nikkei opens lower

Japan's third-quarter GDP contracted 0.1%, the first decline in a year, with high energy and oil prices weighing. The Nikkei 225 opened down 0.63% and the Topix 0.24%; Korea's Kospi was off 0.1% (CNBC, opening moves).

Why it matters: A shrinking economy makes it harder for the Bank of Japan to raise rates to support the yen, which sits near 160 per dollar with Tokyo warning it may intervene.
